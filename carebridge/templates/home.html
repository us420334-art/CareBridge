How CareBridge Works

Requesting care is simple, secure and designed to help you receive support quickly.

1

Create an Account

Register as an Elderly User, Mobility Assistance User, Hearing Assistance User, Caregiver, Volunteer or Care Representative.

2

Submit a Service Request

Describe your care requirements and submit your request through the platform.

3

Representative Review

Care Representatives review your request and assign the most suitable helper.

4

Receive Care

A caregiver or volunteer provides assistance and updates the service status.
